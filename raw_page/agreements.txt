HUAWEI 2026 FINLAND TECH ARENA RULES
2026 Huawei Finland Tech Arena
(the “Challenge”)
CHALLENGE RULES
(the “Rules”)
This Challenge (the "Challenge") is sponsored by Huawei Technologies Oy (Finland) Co. Ltd, Läkkisepäntie 23, 00620 Helsinki, Finland (the "Sponsor"). These rules (the "Rules") set out the terms and conditions governing participation in the Challenge. By participating, you fully and unconditionally agree to comply with these Rules. If you do not agree with any of them, do not register for the Challenge and do not submit an entry.
1. Term
The Challenge runs from 29 October 2026 to 11 December 2026. All dates and times in these Rules refer to Helsinki time (EET, GMT+2).

The Challenge is accessible 24 hours a day on the dedicated website operated by Agorize at https://huawei.agorize.com/en/challenges/2026-finland-tech-arena [https://huawei.agorize.com/en/challenges/2026-finland-tech-arena] (the "Challenge Website"), subject to IT maintenance. The exact opening time of submissions on 9 November 2026 will be announced on the Challenge Website. Any change to these dates will be made in accordance with Section 12.
2. Agreeing to the Rules
Any eligible person who is registered on the Challenge website and enrols to participate in the Challenge ("Participant" or "You"), is required to review and accept these Rules. By accessing and accepting these Rules, You:
(i) enter into a valid and enforceable contractual relationship with the Sponsor regarding participation in the Challenge. The registration and enrolment in the Challenge does not create any subordination relationship between the Sponsor and the Participant, and
(ii) fully and unconditionally agree to comply with these Rules. Participants express their agreement by checking an online box while enrolling for the Challenge.
In case of non-respect of these Rules, the Participant will be immediately disqualified from the Challenge and no prize will be awarded.
3. Registration and participation
To take part in the Challenge, we must receive your completed registration by 1st December 2026 23:55 pm (CET). To register, the Participant must first have created a user account on the Challenge website, and must have truthfully and accurately completed the required information.
Any registration based on inaccurate, false or incomplete information will result in the Participant's disqualification. Refusing collection, recording and use of their personal data that is strictly necessary to performing the Challenge will result in the Participant's disqualification. The Participant is solely responsible for the information he/she provides when registering. Any intentional or unintentional mistake, anomaly or inconsistency, regarding this information, may result in the Participant's disqualification. The Sponsor reserves the right to proceed with all necessary verifications regarding the Participant's identity.
Deletion of a Participant's user account on the Challenge website will be deemed as a withdrawal from the Challenge. In this case, the Participant, regardless of whether the deletion results from the Participant's personal action or not, will not participate in the Challenge, and will not obtain any compensation.
You must also have accepted the Rules as well as the Terms and Conditions of Use [http://www.agorize.com/terms] and the Privacy Policy [http://www.agorize.com/privacy], during the creation of your user account and your registration to the Challenge.
Registration for and participation in the Challenge is free, with no purchase or payment obligation.
4. Eligibility
This Challenge is open and offered solely to:
Students. Any individual aged eighteen (18) years or over and with full legal capacity, who (i) is currently enrolled at a post-secondary institution located in Europe or (ii) has recently graduated from a post-secondary institution located in Europe less than 2 years before the current academic year. Each Student may only participate once in each Challenge.
All the documents that may confirm the Participant's eligibility may be required prior to awarding any prizes (e.g., identity document etc.).
Employees of Huawei and Agorize and/or affiliates, and members of their families, as well as anyone who has directly or indirectly contributed to the conception, organization or realization of the Challenge, are ineligible to take part in the Challenge.
This Challenge is void in countries where it is prohibited or restricted by law.
5. Purpose of the Challenge and participation
Participation in the Challenge must be individual (Individual Participant), or as a team.
Individuals. Individual Participants must enter the Challenge in their individual capacities.
Teams. Individual Participants may elect to form a team ("Team") of 1 to 5 members maximum.
The goal of the Challenge is for Participants to submit contributions (or projects) meeting the requirements determined by the Sponsor in the Challenge brief, and that are formalized by deliverables. The brief includes information regarding various deadlines, details of the prizes, Sponsor requirements etc. (the "Brief"). The Brief is accessible through the Challenge website.
6. Deliverables
During the Challenge, Participants may upload any documents or other materials (such as texts, presentations, videos, etc.) relating to their project to the Challenge website in response to and as a solution to the Sponsor's Challenge (the "Deliverable").
To be eligible, Deliverables must (i) address the specific issue set out in the Brief, (ii) be in a common digital format, such as, PY, ONNX, ZIP, DOC, DOCX, PDF, PPT, PPTX, KEY, ODT, MP3, MPEG, MOV, MP4, and (iii) be in English, and in general terms must comply with these Rules.
If a Deliverable cannot be downloaded, is not in the right format or is incompatible, illegible, or unintelligible, the Deliverable will be disqualified.
By submitting a Deliverable, the following needs must be respected:
The Deliverable includes exclusive contributions from an Individual Participant, or Team.
Otherwise, if content from a third party has been used in the development of the Deliverable, all rights, authorizations and agreements necessary to submit the Deliverable and grant the rights mentioned herein have been obtained. The existence of uncredited third-party contributions in the Deliverable will result in the disqualification of the Deliverable and the related Participants;
No other individual and/or entity is entitled to claim any rights from the use of the Deliverable; and
The content of the Deliverable does not and will not infringe or violate any rights of any third party or entity, including, without limitation, intellectual property rights, privacy, competition law, confidentiality, or any contractual or extracontractual right. All deliverables suspected of violating any law(s) and/or any third party's rights will be ineligible.
Participants are responsible for and shall bear any costs or expenses associated with preparing and submitting Deliverables. Participants assume all risk for damaged, lost, late, incomplete, invalid, incorrect or misdirected Deliverables.
7. Challenge process
The Challenge consists of two rounds: an Online Round, scored automatically, and an Onsite Final in Helsinki, judged by a jury of Huawei experts.
A Team that has not uploaded at least one valid Deliverable by the end of the Online Round is deemed to have withdrawn from the Challenge and cannot claim any compensation from the Sponsor.
The Challenge relies on the creativity, skills and ingenuity of Participants. It does not depend, even partially, on chance and is not a lottery. Factors such as appearance, religion, trade union membership, political opinions or sexual orientation are never taken into account.
A. Online Round
Submission period. Deliverables can be submitted from 9 November 2026 until 1 December 2026, 23:55 (CET).
Submission format. Submissions must follow the technical requirements described in the Brief (submission format, allowed packages and versions, maximum file size, CPU, memory and run-time limits). In case of inconsistency between these Rules and the Brief on technical requirements, the Brief prevails.
Number of submissions. Each Team may submit up to three (3) Deliverables per day. Days run from 00:00 to 23:59 Paris time.
Scoring. Each valid submission is scored automatically on a validation dataset that is not shared with Participants, according to the metric described in the Brief. Scores are shown on a leaderboard (the "Leaderboard") during the Online Round.
Online Round ranking. Each Team is ranked on its best valid submission on the Leaderboard. Ties are broken by the earliest submission time of the tied submissions. A submission that fails to run or exceeds the limits set in the Brief is not valid and receives no score.
B. Selection of finalists
Up to ten (10) Teams, taken in order of their Leaderboard ranking, are selected as finalists, subject to the verification described in Section 7.E. A Team that fails verification or does not confirm its attendance is replaced by the next-ranked Team.
Finalists will be notified by email at the address provided at registration by 4 December 2026. The Sponsor may change the date on which results are released if the number of Deliverables to assess requires it.
C. Onsite Final
The Onsite Final takes place on 11 December 2026 in Helsinki, Finland. The venue and programme will be communicated to finalists with their notification.
Finalists must confirm their attendance within the deadline given in the notification. The Sponsor may, at its sole discretion, disqualify a finalist who does not confirm attendance, without any compensation. Finalists travelling to the Onsite Final from outside Helsinki can have their travel and accommodation costs reimbursed up to EUR 500 per person, against receipts. Details of the reimbursement process will be shared with finalist Teams after selection.
Each finalist Team presents its solution in a pitch session. Before the deadline communicated in the notification, Teams must upload to the Challenge Website (i) their Python project, which is used to assess the novelty of the solution and the use of AI tools, and (ii) a PowerPoint presentation, used for the pitch, describing their design choices and the rationale behind them.
The jury, composed of Huawei experts, evaluates each finalist's solution and pitch on four criteria: technical quality, creativity, completeness, and real-world applicability. The jury may take the Online Round results into account when assessing technical quality. At the end of the Onsite Final, the jury ranks the finalists and selects the winners. The ranking is announced on the day of the Onsite Final.
D. AI tools, external data and fair play
AI tools and libraries. AI coding assistants and open-source libraries are allowed. Pretrained models must be declared in the Deliverable.
External data. Only the data provided by the Sponsor may be used to train and validate solutions; external data is not allowed. Using any copy or source of the evaluation data, or attempting to identify, reconstruct or obtain it, is prohibited.
Fair play. Any attempt to access the evaluation data, to tamper with or exploit the scoring environment, to exceed the submission limit (for example through multiple accounts), or to share code or submissions between Teams results in the disqualification of the Teams involved.
E. Verification of finalists
Before the Onsite Final, shortlisted Teams must provide, on request, their full source code, training scripts and a description of any pretrained models used. Their Leaderboard score must be reproducible, within a reasonable tolerance, from these elements. A Team that cannot provide them, or whose score cannot be reproduced, may be disqualified.
