Shape the future of AI in Health at the Huawei 2026 Finland Tech Arena!
Join us in the Activity Detection From Physiology-Only Data competition and let your innovative solutions shape the future of media technology.
This is your moment to showcase your Bio signal and time-series processing, machine learning, and feature engineering skills, learn from industry experts, and compete for exciting prizes and internship opportunities.
This is your moment to shine and make a difference. Don't miss out on this incredible opportunity to contribute to a high-stakes, high-impact field.
Sign up now and take your first step toward transforming the health tech landscape!
