Shape the future of AI in Health at the Huawei 2026 Finland Tech Arena!

Join us in the Activity Detection From Physiology-Only Data competition and let your innovative solutions shape the future of media technology. 

 

This is your moment to showcase your Bio signal and time-series processing, machine learning, and feature engineering skills, learn from industry experts, and compete for exciting prizes and internship opportunities.

This is your moment to shine and make a difference. Don't miss out on this incredible opportunity to contribute to a high-stakes, high-impact field.

 

Sign up now and take your first step toward transforming the health tech landscape!

On-site Final

One week after finalists are announced. Stay tuned!

  Selected teams will pitch live in front of a Huawei Jury Panel in Helsinki. 

The top finalist teams can win prizes from a cash pool of €16,500, internship opportunities, gain certificates, and many souvenirs!

Venue: Helsinki, Finland

 


Prizes & Incentives

 
Pitch your solutions for the chance to be rewarded by Huawei during the Final Hackathon:

  • First Prize

  • Second Prize

  • Third Prize

  •  Honors

  • Internship Opportunities!

 A cash prize of €6,000 will be awarded to one first-prize team

A cash prize of €3,000 will be awarded to two second-prize teams

A cash prize of €1,500 will be awarded to three third-prize teams

All winners will receive a winning certificate, and the winning teams will receive corresponding certificates.

Winners will have the opportunity to obtain an internship at Huawei.
